## The Cameras Went Dark

At midnight, the warehouse cameras went dark.

Daniel was the only guard on duty.

The rule was simple, never open the dock after midnight.

## Three Knocks

At 12:07, someone knocked three times on the steel door.

The monitors showed only an empty corridor.

The knock came again, slower, like a patient hand.

He took his flashlight and walked the long aisle.

## The Radio That Was Off

His radio crackled, and a voice said, do not answer.

Daniel froze, because the radio had been off all night.

Then footsteps followed him, soft, matching his own.

He turned, but the aisle was empty.

## Footprints Toward the Dock

Fresh footprints appeared, leading toward the dock door.

The footprints stopped, and the knocking stopped too.

He pressed his ear to the steel and heard his own voice.

It whispered, let me in, I am still on shift.

He stepped back as the lights went out, one by one.

---

By morning, the dock door stood open, and Daniel was gone.

They say a new guard now knocks three times, waiting for someone to let him in.
