## A Car Stopped at Midnight

At midnight, on an empty road, a car stopped for a man no one would ever see again.

The driver smiled, but his eyes never left the road.

"Get in," the driver said. "The next town is far."

Mark climbed in and thanked him.

## The Mirror Angled Toward the Back Seat

The car smelled of old leather, and something sweet.

They drove in silence through the fog.

Mark asked where he was heading, but the man never answered.

Then he noticed the mirror, angled toward the back seat.

Someone was sitting there, perfectly still, in a dark coat.

Mark turned around, but the back seat was empty.

The driver laughed softly and said, "He does not talk much."

## The Doors Were Locked

Mark reached for the door handle with shaking hands.

The doors were locked, and the windows would not move.

In the mirror again, the figure was closer, leaning forward.

Mark shouted to stop, but the driver only drove faster.

Then a voice from the back whispered, "Thank you for taking my place."

---

They say the car still stops for lone travellers on that road.

So if a stranger offers you a lift at night, check the back seat, because someone in that car is already waiting for you.
