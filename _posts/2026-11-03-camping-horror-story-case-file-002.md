## Three Campers Went Into the Pines

Three campers went into the pines last summer.

None of them stayed until morning.

## The Footsteps With No Tracks

The first camper heard footsteps circling his tent.

Night after night, they came around the canvas, slow and close. But every morning, the ground was completely untouched. No footprints. No broken twigs. Nothing.

On the third night, the footsteps stopped right beside his head.

And a voice whispered his name from outside the canvas.

## The Campfire Burning Blue

The second camper woke to find her campfire burning blue.

In the flames, she saw a figure sitting where no one had sat before. It did not move, and it cast no shadow.

When she looked away, the figure was gone — but the fire was still blue.

## Three Tents in a Perfect Circle

The third camper followed wet footprints from his tent.

They led deep into the trees, where the moonlight simply stopped. There, he found three tents in a perfect circle, all empty.

Inside each one, a sleeping bag was warm.

He ran back, but his tent was gone, and the path had closed.

---

They say the forest keeps what it is given.

So if you camp in the pines, count your tents at dawn, and never answer a voice that knows your name.
