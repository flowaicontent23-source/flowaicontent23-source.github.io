## The Forest Went Silent

Do not answer if the woods call your name.

Marcus had hiked the Blackpine trail a dozen times alone.

That evening, the forest went silent all at once.

## A Voice Like His Brother

Then a voice called his name from between the trees.

It sounded like his brother, who had died years ago.

Marcus froze, because no one else was on the trail.

The voice called again, closer now, patient and warm.

He shouted back, asking who was there.

Only his own echo answered him from the dark pines.

## Footsteps Around the Camp

His phone showed no signal, and the trail markers were gone.

Footsteps circled his camp, slow, stopping when he moved.

He lit his lantern, but the light would not reach the trees.

## The Whisper Behind His Ear

Then the voice whispered his name right behind his ear.

He turned, and the clearing was empty.

A second voice joined it, then a third, all calling his name.

Marcus ran until his legs gave out beneath the tall pines.

---

By morning, rangers found his tent, still warm, and no Marcus.

They say the woods still call the names of hikers who walk alone.
