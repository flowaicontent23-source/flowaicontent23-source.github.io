## The House on Willow Lane

The house on Willow Lane had been empty for eleven years.

No one lived there, no lights burned, and no one ever knocked.

But every night at three, a light moved behind the curtains.

## The Door Locked From the Inside

Daniel was hired to check the house before it was sold.

The front door was locked from the inside.

He found dust on every floor, except for one clean path to the stairs.

Upstairs, a chair was pulled out, as if someone had just stood up.

A cup on the table was still warm.

He called out, but only his own echo answered.

## Footsteps With No Floor Above

Then he heard footsteps above him, slow and careful.

There was no floor above him.

In the bedroom, the wardrobe door stood open.

Inside, the clothes were new, and one coat was still damp from rain.

On the mirror, someone had written in dust: do not stay after dark.

## Every Room Had Changed

Daniel ran for the door, but every room had changed behind him.

The hallway was longer now, and the front door was gone.

---

They say the house is still empty.

So if you pass Willow Lane at night, and a light moves in the window, do not knock, because someone inside is already waiting for you.
